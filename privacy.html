Privacy Policy

NanoClaw Home is a personal, self-hosted application used only by its owner.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app accesses the owner's Google Tasks and Google Calendar through Google APIs, to read, create and update tasks and events at the owner's request.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

NanoClaw Home's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
